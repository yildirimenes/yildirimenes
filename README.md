### 👨‍💻 Who I Am

I'm a Mobile Developer with 3 years of experience shipping production apps across native and cross-platform stacks.
I care about **clean architecture**, **maintainable codebases**, and **shipping quality software end-to-end** — from the first commit to production rollout.

Currently building mobile products across iOS, Android, tvOS and Android TV, and owning the release process end-to-end.

---

### 🚀 What I Build

- Native Android apps with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose
- Native iOS apps with Swift and SwiftUI
- Cross-platform products with React Native and Kotlin Multiplatform
- Real-time systems (WebRTC video, Socket.IO messaging) and end-to-end delivery pipelines

---

### 🧭 What I Focus On

- **Clean architecture** — MVVM, modularization, and separation of concerns across every stack
- **Cross-platform delivery** — shipping to iOS, Android, tvOS, and Android TV from shared codebases
- **Release ownership** — CI/CD, code-signing, TestFlight and Play Console rollouts
- **Content** — writing about mobile engineering on [ComposeCrafting](http://www.youtube.com/@ComposeCrafting)
